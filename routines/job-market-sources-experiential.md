## 🔴 WHY THIS SEGMENT WAS INVISIBLE, AND IT IS THE SAME DEFECT TWICE BEFORE

**Every board in `job-market-sources.md` is venue-bound by nature.** Theatre boards list jobs at theatres.
So agency-side and exhibit-side technical drafting — which is real, salaried, often freelance, and
sometimes remote — **has never once been swept by this routine.**

That is the third instance of one pattern:

1. **The Department-Index Law** — three lanes read 0-4 for weeks; one department index took them to 12, 9
   and 4. The market had not changed. We had never opened the page.
2. **The Safety Sweep** — safety keywords returned zero across every board for weeks and were proposed for
   deletion. They post on venue/arena/municipal boards, not theatre boards. The keywords were fine.
3. **This segment** — `drafting-design` has carried a 2-6 row band and a standing suspicion of being "two
   theatres staffing a season." It was being measured against a source list that structurally cannot see
   half its market.

⭐ **The generalizable rule, now three-for-three: a lane returning nothing is a statement about our
SOURCES, never about the world.** Before believing a market is thin, prove the sweep was pointed somewhere
it could possibly have succeeded.

## Specialist boards — a theatre sweep will never surface these

Treat as **Tier 2** for `drafting-design`, and opportunistic for `production-manager` and `creative-admin`.

- [**IAEE Career Center**](https://www.iaee.com/careers/) `IAEE` — International Association of Exhibitions
  and Events. Exhibition-industry hiring rather than theatre.
- [**Exhibition Industry Jobs**](https://www.exhibitionindustryjobs.com/) `EIJ` — dedicated exhibition/
  trade-show board.
- [**Tradeshow Job Board**](https://tradeshowjobboard.com/) `TSJB` — trade-show industry roles.
- [**SEGD Job Bank**](https://jobs.segd.org/) `SEGD` — Society for Experiential Graphic Design. Exhibit and
  environmental design; skews design-side but carries technical roles.
- [**EDPA Future Workforce**](https://www.edpa.com/future-workforce-events) `EDPA` — Experiential Designers
  and Producers Association. ⭐ Its own workforce page **names CAD engineers, CNC operators, riggers and
  electricians** as the industry's open roles and states the industry trains on the job. That is an
  explicit entry route for a theatre-credentialed candidate with no agency history.
- [**Cad Crowd**](https://www.cadcrowd.com/jobs/search) `CC` — freelance CAD marketplace. ⚠️ Mostly
  architectural/mechanical; entertainment work is sporadic. Filter Remote and keyword Vectorworks /
  Spotlight / rigging / event / scenic / structural. Low yield, cheap to check.
- **LinkedIn saved searches** — the highest-yield door in this segment, and the only one that reliably
  surfaces freelance remote drafting. Search: `Vectorworks Spotlight` · `event CAD` · `experiential
  fabrication` · `freelance technical designer` · `exhibit CAD detailer` · `AV CAD designer`.

### Pay, verbatim only

Never quote an aggregator's estimate as market rate.

- Unboxed Experiential Technical Designer — **"$90,000 - $125,000 / yr"** · names Vectorworks · hybrid LA
- TAIT / Gallagher Production Designer/Drafter — **"$100,000-120,000 per annum"**
- NVE Experiential Designer — **"$80,000.00-$105,000.00 per year"**
- PRG Technical Designer — **"$65,000.00-85,000.00"**
- Encore CAD Specialist II — **"Hourly Pay Range: $24.98 - $32.38"**
- Freelance range seen on real postings — **$30-40/hr** at the low end, **$35-75/hr** on the closed Jack
  Morton role, **$75-85/hr** on a contractor Experiential Designer role
- Occupational benchmark — BLS *Set and Exhibit Designers*, 2024 median **$66,280 / $31.87 per hour**,
  and ⭐ **64% self-employed**, which is the strongest single piece of evidence that this segment is
  freelance-heavy by structure rather than by accident

### 🔴 REMOTE IS THE EXCEPTION THAT GETS ADVERTISED, NOT THE NORM

**Do not oversell this.** Remote freelance drafting is real and it is a minority. Most verified employer
listings in this segment require office or hybrid attendance, site surveys, shop visits, fabrication
coordination, load-ins, travel, or a local hub presence. Unboxed wants 3 office days; Encore says
*"primarily in an office environment"*; Derse required Dallas-area residence; PRG needs site and
fabrication support; ATOMIC expects up to 40% travel.

⚠️ **So a pass that finds few remote rows here is reporting the market correctly.** Say "swept, remote is
structurally scarce in this segment" — never "no remote work exists."

### 🚨 THE MIRROR TRAP, with a live case from the day this file was written

This segment is **aggregator-heavy**, far more than the theatre boards, so the parent file's
mirror-is-not-a-source rule bites harder here.

**The case:** a "Technical Production Drafting — CAD" role at Jack Morton surfaced only on
`willow.2kool4u.net`, a mirror. It was NOT fabricated — it had a genuine Greenhouse job ID and the mirror
even reproduced Jack Morton's own scam warning and accommodation email. **But the job ID is absent from
Jack Morton's live Greenhouse feed, so the role is CLOSED.** The mirror still renders it as current, with
a salary and a remote claim.

🔴 **And the failure that matters: one sweep in the 2026-09-18 pass promoted that mirror URL into its main
table as a live row, while a parallel sweep correctly marked the same listing unverifiable.** Two reads of
one listing, one right. It was caught before it was rowed, by Michael asking about it.

**The rule, restated for this segment: an ATS job ID that no longer appears in the employer's live feed is
CLOSED, however current the mirror looks.** Check the employer's own ATS feed — Greenhouse, iCIMS, Jobvite,
Workday, Dover, Paylocity — not the aggregator.

### Entry route for a theatre-credentialed candidate

The honest order, easiest door first: **event CAD / production drafter → AV CAD designer → rigging or
Braceworks designer → production designer for stage structures → associate technical designer → freelance
event CAD support → exhibit CAD detailing LAST** (and gated on the AutoCAD ruling above).

The differentiator is a portfolio that proves fabrication literacy, not a credential: scaled venue and
stage plans, rigging plots with real Braceworks calculation output, load paths and hoist/truss
documentation, AV and lighting layouts, one or two shop-ready scenic details, visible revision control.

⚠️ **Vectorworks Spotlight certification exists (~$249, three-year validity) but is optional and is NOT a
substitute for the portfolio.** Noted for completeness, not recommended.
