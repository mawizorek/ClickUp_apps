Shoe-box

Prints you own that aren't in the binder — either not at all, or not all of them.

Nothing on this page is stored — it's worked out from what you own minus what's placed, so it can't drift from your collection. Spares are arithmetic, not a ledger: the app knows you own six and one is on a sheet, so five are in the box. It does not track which copy is where, and it doesn't need to.
