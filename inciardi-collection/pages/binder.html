Reading a sheet

Nine slots a face, a front and a back to every sheet. Owned and wanted are worked out from what you actually have, so they can never disagree with your collection.

OwnedYou have it, and it's in this slot.
WantedLaid out, not yet owned. The visible gap.
NoteA reminder in a slot instead of a print.
EmptyNothing recorded. Tap to fill it.

Swipe, arrow keys or ‹ › turn one face at a time, straight through the binder.

A print can sit in more than one slot. When it does, the card spells out own N · placed M so it can never read as owning more than you do.
