Binder

Nine slots a side, two sides a sheet. Tap any slot to fill or change it.

Dotted = empty, nothing recorded. Dashed = a print you want but don't own yet. Solid = owned. Owned and wanted are worked out from what you actually have, so they can never disagree with your collection. A print may sit in more than one slot — the card always reads own N · placed M so that can't read as owning more than you do.
