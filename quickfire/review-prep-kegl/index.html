cue 4 — the standby list

Five questions, in this order.

01
Which track is this review on?

The public directory lists you as Senior Lecturer / Production Manager. Faculty review and staff review have different criteria, readers and outcomes. Everything depends on this.

02
What are the criteria, and who reads the file?

A teaching-shaped rubric cannot see a production operation. If the readers are English faculty, the dossier has to explain the operation, not assume it.

03
Can the production scope go in as reviewable work?

Four direct reports, ten craft disciplines, six production units this season. If it is not in the file it was not reviewed.

04
Is a business-title change in scope here?

UR HR runs five career streams and twelve job levels and permits a school or program business title over the system title. The mechanism exists. Ask where it lives.

05
Deadline, format, who else signs?

The boring question that decides your actual runway. Get it in writing before you leave.
