cue 3 — three gaps, in writing

What neither document gives you.

No reappointment process
Appendix II Section II covers the instructional ranks. Section IV calls teaching-track appointments limited-term and stops. The Handbook's own appointment-and- reappointment section covers instructor and assistant professor only.
No notification guarantee
Handbook IV.A.1 promises written notice a year out to an instructor, assistant professor, associate professor, or professor without tenure. Lecturer and senior lecturer are not on that list. Your letter's renewal sentence is the protection you actually hold.
A definition that doesn't fit
The Handbook's only substantive description of senior lecturer (IV.B.6) sits under Part-Time titles. You are full-time. Appendix II calls it full-time teaching track. Two documents, two framings, no procedure in either.

One principle does apply to you: the Handbook says renewable non-tenure-eligible term appointments should be reviewed periodically so they are not continued unreasonably. An obligation to review, with no procedure attached.

cue 5 — the standby list

Five asks, in this order.

01
Which document governs this review?

Appendix II Section II, the offer letter's renewal clause, or something the department holds. If it is Section II, that section is written for the instructional ranks and you are teaching track — so which is it.

02
Can I have the department's written governance rules?

If a process for a senior lecturer exists anywhere, it lives there. You are entitled to a copy, not a summary.

Handbook § II.F — every academic unit "should have a set of written rules and procedures for its governance, with copies available to each faculty member"
03
What are the criteria, and who reads the file?

A teaching-shaped rubric cannot see a production operation. If the readers are English faculty, the dossier has to explain the operation rather than assume it. And ask whether the production scope goes in as reviewable work at all — if it is not in the file, it was not reviewed.

04
Is conversion to the instructional track on the table?

Senior Lecturer converts directly to Associate Professor of Instruction. Administrative process, chair's recommendation required, no automatic raise. It moves you onto a track that has a written procedure — which is the whole problem above, solved.

05
Deadline, format, who signs, and may I review my file?

The boring questions that decide your runway. Get the deadline in writing before you leave, and put the file request in writing separately.

Handbook § IV.I — written request, chair for department files, dean for school files. Confidential material is removed and indexed.
