Three measured counterexamples. The rule is a default, not a truth.

20501 · General scenic tools landed in SC61200 on a McMaster supplier invoice ($8.43, $31.27) and SC56650 Other Expenses on a Home Depot P-card ($23.56). Same code, same shop, two different ledger accounts, decided by how it was bought.

20383 · Programs printing should be supplies by every reasonable reading. The university filed $868.49 of opening-night programs under Events, inside Travel and Conference.

10304 · Safety Trainings is a service bought from RIT for $295. It posted as Supplies Other.

So: derive the expected bucket, show it, and let the imported ledger row overwrite it. Never treat the derived value as the reported one.

One invoice can hold two ledger accounts, and that is normal

Supplier invoice SPI06234783 to Ismail khalidi carries $3,750 as a creative fee under 61500 and $421.97 as travel reimbursement under 61400. One document, one person, two university buckets.

This is why the import unit is the line, not the invoice, and why splits have to be first-class. One Business Document in the March export carried more than thirty separate costume lines.

03

Where the rule stops, on purpose

Eight subcategories resolve an operating line but never an expense bucket.

A reserve has no expense type

These are reserves and catch-alls. The spend inside them is arbitrary by design. In the March export alone, 20310 Contingency bought McMaster safety hardware ($38.03, $34.09, $19.13, $28.24) and front-of-house miscellany ($97.01). Nothing predicts the next one.

  • PRD — Contingency reserve
  • PRD — PM Discretionary reserve
  • PRD — Miscellaneous catch-all
  • PRG — Miscellaneous catch-all
  • FST — Production whole-event bucket
  • FST — Contingency reserve
  • SHR — Other Production Support catch-all
  • UTL — Needs to Code undecided by definition

Show them as unassigned until a transaction lands. They are also where the money is least visible, which argues for watching them rather than hiding them. Header rows are a separate class: PRD — Header and its siblings hold no spend at all, so they need no bucket. They are subtotal lines.
