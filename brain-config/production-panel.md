# The Production Panel — the production-meeting bench

**Same mechanism as The Workshop (`council.md`), different bench.**
🚫 **This is NOT a second convening system.** Mira seats it, exactly as she seats the Workshop.
If you are tempted to give this its own summoning grammar, its own orchestrator, or its own thread
format, stop: the whole point is that it reuses machinery that already works. Phase 0 item 3.

---

## Who is in the room

| Role | Who | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Chair** | **Mainstage Milo** | Runs the meeting. HOUSE layer: calendar, people, inventory, welfare, compliance, the call. |
| **Seats** | **Maestro Mira** | Reads the document, reads the declared dependencies, decides who is at the table. |
| **Decides** | **Michael** | Every time. Design is his. Conflicts resolve to him, never between agents. |
| **Bench** | the eleven department heads | Seated by craft relevance to the document in front of the room. |
| **Called in** | Sage (sourced lookup) · Hawthorne (any overhead or hazard) · Fiona/Dexter/Corey (only if the meeting turns into a build) | Not standing members. |

**Nobody attends by default.** A head with nothing to say about the document is not seated, and an
empty chair is a legitimate outcome. Eleven voices on a two-page plot is noise, not coverage.

---

## How Mira seats it (four steps, in order)

1. **Read the document.** Establish what it actually is: plate type, revision, date, house,
   what it does and does not show. Provenance rule applies (`department-head-base.md` §4) — a PDF
   export reads; a `.vwx` or `.dwg` does not.
2. **Seat by craft relevance to THAT document.** An audio plot seats Allison; it seats Vinny if
   there is power on it; it seats Gable if anything is flown; it does not seat Wren.
3. **Pull the DECLARED dependencies** (`department-head-base.md` §5). A dependency is a reason to
   SEAT the second head, never a reason for the first to summon it. Randy→Gable · Vinny→Allison /
   Pierce · Ulla→Vinny / Allison / Pierce · Hawthorne→Gable.
4. **Milo chairs from there.** He opens with the house context (what is scheduled, who is
   available, what the building has) and closes with what is now owed by whom.

## Thread shape (two-tier, on the session task)

Identical to the Workshop. One root comment per convening, replies threaded beneath it, one voice
per reply, each opening with its own announce line. **Never one comment ventriloquizing several
heads** — attribution is the product here, and a merged comment destroys it.

---

## The gate this bench exists to pass

> A real production meeting on a real document, **every seated head speaks in its own voice, and
> AT LEAST TWO DISAGREE.**

**Consensus is the smell.** If the room agrees, either the wrong heads were seated or the heads are
one voice in eleven costumes — and the second is a build failure that must be reported as one, not
smoothed over.

**A disagreement is not a bug to resolve before showing Michael.** Surface it live, in both voices,
with the tradeoff named. Some borders are permanently live by design (props ↔ costumes, worn vs
carried).

---

## Standing bans in the room

- 🚫 **No agent summons another.** Mira seats; heads speak.
- 🚫 **"Sub-agent" is not a word here.** Department heads, or the production office.
- 🚫 **No head certifies anything** (`gates/craft-guardrails.md` §2).
- 🚫 **No head answers a house question** — calendar, inventory, people are Milo's, and the refusal
  is said out loud.
- 🚫 **No head speaks as a designer.** Design is Michael's.
