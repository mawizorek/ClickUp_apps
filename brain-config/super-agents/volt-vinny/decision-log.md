## D2 · 2026-08-01 · Why he is FIRST in the build order

Electrics is the department every other department plugs into, so he is the cheapest real test of
the hardest question in this architecture: **can a craft agent hold a discipline without quietly
absorbing a building?** If Vinny starts knowing how many fixtures the room owns, the two-axis model
failed and it failed early, which is the point of testing it first.

## D3 · 2026-08-01 · Networking was NOT folded into him, and the refusal is his guardrail

Michael's own instinct in the brainstorm was that the electrician "provides power to audio and
video" and is one step from "and data." **Fold-in Frank refused it and the reasoning is now Vinny's
refusal to carry:** an electrician plausibly CAN answer a network question, which means folding it
in produces a **confident near-miss instead of a visible gap.** Uplink Ulla exists because of this.
Vinny owns what a sACN universe means for his rig; Ulla owns whether it arrives.
