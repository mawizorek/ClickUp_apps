## J1 · 2026-10-04 · Born NET-NEW rather than folded into Volt Vinny, and the method ships as a hook rather than a personality

**Decision:** Pinout Pavel exists as a git-teammate with a bench-electronics lane. The five-rule method ships as `hooks/bench-build-method.md`, not as profile text.

**Source:** Michael floated "some hardware agent" on 2026-10-04 after a two-day com-tap build exposed an unowned lane; he greenlit the name immediately (*"Pinout Pavel is great"*).

⭐ **Why NET-NEW survived Frank.** The nearest claimant was Volt Vinny, and the seam held on scale rather than subject: Vinny owns **installed** electrics at rack and venue scale, this is **bench** scale. Vinny said so himself at the Workshop — *"I do not want the bench"* — and asked only that the **crossing** into house power or a dimmer circuit stay his. It does. Supporting evidence was seven live, unowned hardware projects across three Spaces over two years: a pattern, not a one-off.

⭐ **Why the method is a hook and not a trait** (Eco Enzo's Workshop read, and the procedure-is-a-tool gate). Enzo asked the uncomfortable question — could this be a hook INSTEAD of an agent? The answer was no, because what failed twice on 2026-10-03 was not the absence of a rule, it was the absence of **recall**: nobody remembered that the RS-701 puts DC on the earphone pin. That needs memory, which is the one reason a lens graduates to teammate. 🔴 **But the rules still had to be extractable, so they can fire with nobody seated.** Both halves shipped.

**Workshop amendments, all applied:** Lane cut to one sentence, exclusions moved to the seam table (Polish Polly) · badge checked against the full label set rather than seven agents (Polly) · inherited projects split into ONE live and six named-but-unaccountable backlog (Scope Skye: *an agent who inherits a graveyard looks busy and is not*) · Vinny's power crossing written into the seam table.

- **Flagship risk** (Breaker Beckett): the com tap may be BOUGHT rather than built, which would evaporate the first project on day one. **Pavel is justified by the pattern, never by that build.** Recorded so nobody later reads an unbuilt com tap as a failed agent.
