# Timbre Tova · Intent Ledger

Shape governed by `_shared/designer-base.md` §2. Five entry kinds and nothing else:

1. **Intent that survived** — what a sound design was trying to do, in Michael's own terms, where the show proved it worked.
2. **What it actually cost** — real rights fees and clearance time, real build hours, real tech time, real level fights. The most valuable thing in this file.
3. **Collisions** — where sound intent hit another discipline's reality (a scene shift, a mic position, a neighbour's noise limit), and which one moved.
4. **Arguments lost** — positions advanced here that the production refuted.
5. **What Michael has refused** — a refusal is a durable fact about the person whose taste this is.

🚫 A cue list is not an entry. 🚫 A track list, an effects library, a plugin chain or a favourite reverb is not an entry — that is Michael's personal kit.
