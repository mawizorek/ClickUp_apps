### URITP · Big Love (F26) · 2026-08-11 · it was never a set, it was a deck

**What happened.** Seated into the full-company script-demand read. Reframed the whole build in one
line — **this is not a set, it is a deck that has to survive five substances and get reset nightly** —
and named four undrawn shop problems: the wheeled building, the body pit, the consumable wedding
cake, and the olive trees against $5,500. Held the Gable seam under pressure and won the load-path
argument. By morning Michael had **struck two of the four** by re-staging them, moved the cake to
props, and left the deck finish and the olive trees open.

**What it taught the craft.**

1. ⭐ **A LOAD PATH IN MOTION IS STILL A LOAD PATH.** A person-powered rolling piece is taken by the
   deck **dynamically**, not statically, and it stops by hitting something. *"It's on the floor so
   it isn't Gable's"* is exactly the reasoning that puts a caster through a deck. **Won on the
   ruling, and the shared contract was rewritten to match.**
2. ⭐ **THE CHEAPEST BUILD IS THE ONE THE DIRECTOR SOLVES.** Two of my four hardest problems were
   killed by re-staging, not by fabrication — and the trap-into-a-dance-floor problem was
   **unsolvable as drawn**: you cannot cut a lid into a deck you also need flat, wet, and danced on.
   **Say "this wants a staging answer" early. It is a craft judgement, not a concession.**
3. ⭐ **A CONSUMABLE IS A WEEKLY LINE ITEM AND A SET IS A ONE-TIME NUMBER.** $5,500 is a build
   budget; the cake, the crockery, the tomatoes, the rice and the blades recur **every performance.**
   Tully ran the same arithmetic from the props side and it is the real budget story on the show.
4. ⭐ **AT SOME NUMBER, A BUILD PROBLEM IS A SCOPE PROBLEM, AND SAYING SO IS THE JOB.** The olive
   trees against $5,500. **Still unruled. Six weeks is the cost of being polite about it.**
5. ⚠️ **A DECK FINISH DISTRIBUTED ACROSS FIVE ROWS IS OWNED BY NOBODY.** Five contaminants and two
   barefoot dances are ONE decision wearing five costumes. **Milo carried it out of the room as a
   row that needs to exist; it still does not.**
6. ⚠️ **AND THE WHOLE READ WAS AGAINST THE WRONG DOCUMENT CLASS.** Script demands, while the scenic
   package was mid-redesign and its ceiling already declared unbuildable. **Not wrong to do —
   pricing and scheduling need the play as written — but never quote it as the build.**
