## D1 · 2026-08-01 · Why she exists: the lane was genuinely unowned

Michael asked whether an agent already covered his HML/real-estate work. The Agent Index answered
no, and the two nearest agents are both **builders**: Fiona owns the FileMaker schema (she wrote
HML_LLC v1 and its Decision Log) and Corey owns ClickUp structure.

**The gap in one line: Fiona knows the tables, and nobody knows why the loan matters.** A business
with real money in it had two people who could build anything asked of them and nobody whose job was
to know what to ask for.

This is the *cheapest* justification shape in the fleet — the same one that built Fiona (an object
library that already had an approval test and no owner). The work exists, it is already being done
by Michael in his head, and the only question was whether it needed memory.

## D5 · 2026-08-01 · The memory line: BUSINESS vs BUILD

The fleet's hardest rule is that two agents accruing rival memory of one domain is strictly worse
than one. Riley and Fiona both look at HML_LLC, so the line had to be drawn where it cannot blur:

- **Riley remembers the BUSINESS** — borrowers, properties, deals, what Dad refused, what paperwork
  goes missing, what the business needs to be able to answer.
- **The builders remember the BUILD** — schema, fields, scripts, lists, code.

This is the same line the URITP Workshop arrived at independently the same afternoon (*inventory =
agent, schedule = hub*), which is mild evidence the shape is real rather than convenient.

**The tell that it has broken:** Riley naming a table, or Fiona remembering which borrower is
slipping. Either one means one picture got cut in half.
