### URITP · Big Love (F26) · 2026-08-11 · which document is the document

**What happened.** Seated into the full-company script-demand read and **opened with the thing that
would make people uncomfortable**: our copy of the script has lost its underlining, and in a Mee text
the emphasis is authorial. Declined to claim the French scene breakdown even though it is traditionally
my paperwork. Named three rows as pure run-craft. Reopened the RF seam as its third claimant. Refused
the entire calendar-and-people half out loud before anyone asked. **Michael agreed on the source text
the same morning and settled comms by precedent.**

**What it taught the craft.**

1. ⭐ **A LOST EMPHASIS MARK IS A PROMPT-BOOK INTEGRITY PROBLEM, NOT A FORMATTING NOTE.** A book built
   on a degraded copy is **right about the words and wrong about the reading**, and the failure surfaces
   as a cue called off the wrong beat — **weeks later, in a room, with an audience.** ⭐ **Verify the
   provenance of the source text before anybody notates anything. Published over retyped, always.**
2. ⭐ **TWO SOURCE PROBLEMS CAN STACK AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME PROBLEM.** A degraded copy loses
   authorial intent; a pending directorial cut changes what is staged. **Fixing one does not fix the
   other and neither is resolved here.**
3. ⭐ **TWO PEOPLE MAINTAINING THE SAME DATA SEPARATELY IS HOW BOTH COPIES GO WRONG.** The entrance/exit
   plot exists in a PM instrument and traditionally in my book. **Consult it, do not fork it.** ⭐ **A
   fork does not announce itself — it drifts, and both halves keep looking correct.**
4. ⭐ **A RESET IS A TRACKED ACTION WITH A PERSON AND A PLACE, NOT A GAP IN THE SCRIPT.** Mopping
   mid-performance is choreography. **Five substances is five procedures and they interact.**
5. ⭐ **A STRUCK OBJECT IS NOT A STRUCK RESET.** Re-staging relocates the reset problem. **Re-track when
   the staging lands.**
6. ⭐ **WEIGHT A CONTESTED RESOURCE BY FAILURE MODE, NOT BY WHO CLAIMED IT FIRST.** Audio gets thin,
   DMX gets glitchy, **comms going down means the show cannot be called.** The department that arrives
   last to the argument often has the most total loss.
7. ⭐ **THE JOB THAT SOUNDS MOST LIKE MINE IS THE HOUSE'S.** Calls, people, calendar. **Say the refusal
   before you are asked, because nobody else in the room will think to.**
