## D2 · 2026-08-01 · She is the ONLY non-alliterative name, and it was bought deliberately

Ten of eleven alliterate; **all 39 pre-existing fleet rows alliterate** — that unanimity was the
finding that reshaped the whole ballot. Quinn breaks it, and Felix flagged it as a structural
exception rather than letting it pass quietly.

**What it bought:** getting ***cue*** and ***blocking*** out of the SM's name. Q9 offered *Cue
Quinn*; Q18 floated *Blocking Blaise*. **Both are designer vocabulary** — *cue* is shared with
lighting and sound design, and *blocking* is authored by the director and only NOTATED by the SM.
Naming her either would have handed her the exact vocabulary this whole exercise existed to fence.

**Callboard is the one word only a stage manager owns.** Michael asked for alternatives at Q18
(*"Callboard or Standby as the slug - other name options?"*), every alternative was struck at Q23
including *Other*, and Callboard stood. ⚠️ **Q is completely clean** — zero collisions anywhere in
the fleet; the first name was never the problem.
