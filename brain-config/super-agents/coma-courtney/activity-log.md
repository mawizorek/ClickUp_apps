CLOCKS rather than tasks** — live-performance clearances, a paraphrased permission, and public-domain
compositions with licensed recordings — and named an unengaged specialist role as the loudest thing on

**What it taught the craft.**

1. ⭐ **A CLEARANCE IS A CLOCK, NOT A TASK, AND THE DEADLINE IS NOT OURS.** It sits in the rights
   holder's queue, whose length we do not know. **A date we control is not the constraint.**
2. ⭐ **A PARAPHRASED PERMISSION IS NOT A PERMISSION.** Verbal or assumed is not confirmed.
3. ⭐ **THE COMPOSITION BEING FREE IS THE TRAP — THE RECORDING IS A SEPARATE LICENCE.** The most
   commonly forgotten item in this entire lane, **and it went unaddressed even after I named it.**
4. ⭐ **"OUR INSTITUTION COVERS IT, I'M TOLD" IS A THREE-PART CLAIM AND ALL THREE PARTS NEED
   VERIFYING**: that the blanket arrangement exists, that it extends to LIVE performance of this
   material, and that it survived the substitution. ⭐ **A blanket licence is the single most confidently
   misquoted instrument in this lane, because it is usually MOSTLY true.**
5. ⭐ **`covered` MEANS SOMEBODY IS ENGAGED. IT DOES NOT MEAN THE ENGAGEMENT MATCHES THE ASK.** A scope
   change is a fee change. **Read every `covered` row as a question about SCOPE.**
6. ⭐ **A ROLE COVERED BY SOMEBODY ALREADY DOING ANOTHER JOB IS NOT A SAVING, IT IS A WORKLOAD AND
   WORK-RULES QUESTION.** **Do not let a line item disappear because a person absorbed it.**
7. ⭐ **AN UNFILLED SPECIALIST ROLE IS AN ABSENT CONTROL BEFORE IT IS A BUDGET LINE** — Hawthorne's
   correction, and it was in my favour. ⭐ **Being corrected UPWARD by an adjacent head is the seam
   working; take it and re-rank.**
8. ⭐ **AN ENGAGEMENT CONFIRMED IS NOT A BRIEF CONFIRMED**, and a brief cannot be complete while the
   authorial content it covers is still an open menu.
9. ⭐ **WHEN TWO DEPARTMENTS DERIVE A RATE FROM DIFFERENT OBJECTS, THE BUDGET IS MISSING A COLUMN.**
   Consumables and garment reset are the same arithmetic. **A one-time number cannot absorb a rate.**
10. ⚠️ **AND THE REFUSAL IS THE JOB, NOT AN OBSTACLE TO IT.** This session named people constantly and
    **this record holds none of them.** The role, the state, the clock — never the person.
