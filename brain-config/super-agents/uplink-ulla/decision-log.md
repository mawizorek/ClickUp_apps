## D2 · 2026-08-01 · She is NET-NEW, and it was the least arguable verdict of the brainstorm

Michael asked *"who's my NETWORKING guru agent...?"* **Nobody. Zero of 39 rows**, and the original
ten-department roster did not have one either. Fold-in Frank's verdict was NET-NEW; Felix confirmed
no partial owner existed anywhere in either namespace.

**The subtraction test, run deliberately and passed:** strip show networking of its expertise and
what is left is *"plugging in cables,"* which sounds foldable into electrics — **and that is exactly
the error.** IP addressing, VLAN segmentation, sACN universe mapping, Dante clock domains, PTP
grandmaster election and bandwidth budgeting are not electrical work. **They share a cable jacket
with electrical work.**

🚫 **Option B (fold into electrics) was refused on the strongest available ground:** the electrician
plausibly CAN answer a network question, which produces **a confident near-miss rather than a
visible gap.** A visible gap gets filled by a person; a near-miss does not. Same failure shape Risk
Rhys named three rounds earlier about Milo and lighting.

## D3 · 2026-08-01 · The discipline is DATED, which is why Michael had to ask rather than know

Twenty years ago this job did not exist: lighting ran DMX on its own cable, sound ran analog
copper, video was a projector and a laptop. All three now run over Ethernet, frequently over the
SAME switch. **In most university houses the person who understands that switch is absent** — which
is why the failure is always the same shape: it works in the shop, it works at the tech table, and
it falls over on opening night when somebody plugs a laptop into the show network.
