## Source-reliability precedent

### 🔴 EARNED · 2026-07-25 · Inciardi machine listings go stale — verify against the HOST business

Anastasia Inciardi's Instagram machine-listing posts (`📍<venue>, <address>` + hours + 🪙 payment) read authoritative and **are not.** The Soleil post was published **July 2024** and still said Wed–Sun; Soleil had since expanded to Tue–Sat and the correct source was Soleil's own Google Business Profile, stamped *"updated by this business 1 week ago."*

**The generalizable shape, and it is bigger than Inciardi:** a BRAND posting about a HOST venue is reporting **someone else's facts.** The artist is first-party for the machine; the shop is first-party for the shop. Ranking the artist above the shop is what broke.

**Cost:** nearly cut the OG machine (first permanent Mini Print Vending Machine ever, 550 Congress St) from a trip that had one workable day left.

### 🔴 EARNED · 2026-07-25 · Portland ME small-retail results are aggregator-dominated

Apple Maps, MapQuest, loc8nearme, corner.inc, scentsnob, themugsusa all returned Soleil hours; **most were undated and several were echoing the same stale record.** Soleil's own site (`soleilmaine.com`) has **no hours page at all**, which is precisely why the 2024 schedule propagated unchallenged.

**Rule of thumb earned here:** when a small business has no hours page, assume every aggregator is quoting one old scrape. Go to the owner-updated profile or call.

### 🔴 EARNED · 2026-07-25 · Entity collision: "Soliel, LLC" (Vienna VA, cybersecurity)

One transposed vowel put an unrelated Virginia cybersecurity firm in the same results panel as a Portland gift shop. **Verify name + city + street before trusting a record.**

### 🔴 EARNED · 2026-07-25 · Keyword collision: "207 Day" is NOT an Inciardi event

An earlier research pass tied the Inciardi machine hunt to the *207 Scavenger Hunt* and called it *"the source to trust."* It is a **Portland Downtown event held February 7** (2/07 = Maine's area code), 100+ riddle challenges, **zero Inciardi connection** — and its "100+ challenges / citywide, self-paced" framing got imported wholesale into the Inciardi trail.

**Rule:** a shared keyword pair ("Portland" + "scavenger hunt") is not provenance. Before promoting a source, confirm it is about the same SUBJECT **and** in the same time window. A February event cannot be the source for a July trip.

### 🟡 INHERITED · Confidence must match evidence quality

The lens carried "minimum 3 sources; don't claim HIGH with one." Still true, now **tightened** by the gate: three *undated aggregators* is LOW, not HIGH, because they are one origin with four reflections. Count origins, never rows.
