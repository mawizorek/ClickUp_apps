### The renumber — the single most load-bearing fact in my lane

**The department moved from an ENGL prefix to THTR and renumbered nearly every course**, effective
academic year 26-27. Nigel Maister's words to the company, 2025-12-02: *"Theatre courses will now have a
THTR prefix and the vast majority will have a new number too."*

- 🚨 **A BARE THREE-DIGIT NUMBER IS AMBIGUOUS AND RESOLVES WRONG ABOUT HALF THE TIME.**
  ENGL 123 was Playwriting → **THTR 123 is Intro to Lighting.** ENGL 124 was Intro to Lighting →
  **THTR 124 is Intro to Sound.** ENGL 270 was Advanced Technical Theater → **THTR 270 is Topics in
  Performance.** *Always write the prefix. Never accept a bare number from anyone, including Michael.*
- **The mapping is NOT one-to-one, four shapes:** digits reused for a different course · two legacy numbers
  collapsed into one (ENGL 296+297 → THTR 295; 298+299 → THTR 298) · one current number over many legacy
  courses (ENGL 292/293 → THTR 290, sectioned per production) · topics numbers (THTR 260 and 270 carry
  many titles). **So a course number is NOT a course identity** — THTR 290.1 has been four different plays.
- **The old `[124]` / `[123]` / `|124|` list names in the Courses space are ONE course across two numbering
  eras, not inconsistency.** A `THTR 123` folder holding five `[124]` lists is CORRECT.

  sheet, the ClickUp record bodies and the April 2026 student newsletter all agree. **Cross-check a crosswalk
  against three origins before trusting it.**
