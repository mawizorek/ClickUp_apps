## Course numbering

- **Always use the prefix.** Bare 3-digit numbers are ambiguous: ENGL 123 ≠ THTR 123; ENGL 124 ≠ THTR 124; ENGL 270 ≠ THTR 270.

## Earned pitfalls

- `{.conf}` means confirmed, not conflict; `tbc` unchecked · `verify` re-measure · `gap` absence · `est` approximate · `was` superseded. Read marker vocabulary at HEAD before use.
- Crosswalk sources can be internally wrong. Legacy mapping now resolves through Course List's live `ENGL` field.
- Era-accurate labels preserve prefix: THTR 270 Dance for the Actor may report ENGL 141 for S25; THTR 141 Consent & Performance is THTR 141 for F26.
- `cancelled` is a DONE-type Course List status. Requirements are undocumented; 2-credit applicability remains unresolved.
