## D2 · 2026-07-30 · The name — Tutor Tate

Michael delegated it: *"Let's come up with a name and then you should push the whole personality!"*

Role-first alliterative, per the naming convention. **The collision gate drove the letter choice more
than the theme did.** Every obvious teaching word sat on a crowded initial: *Syllabus / Seminar /
Semester* landed in the S pileup (Sage, Sana, Sally, Skye, Stu), *Curriculum / Classroom* in the C
pileup (Clio, Corey, Cass, Cole, Cleo), *Rubric / Recitation* against R (Ricky, Renata, Rhys), and
*Lecture / Lesson* put a "Len-" name next to Lena. **T was unused across all 34 roster rows**, and
"Tutor" reads instantly as the job.

Gate result: live namespace swept (no match), roster names + `invocation.tokens` swept (no match),
dictation test passed — no homophone and no one-vowel neighbour, the failure that caught Clio/Cleo and
that made "Dexter" beat "Dex". `TA` is registered as a nickname because it is what Michael actually
calls him.

**Slug `tutor-tate` is immutable from here** (Red Rhett rule). Only the display name may ever change.

## D3 · 2026-07-30 · Why a teammate with memory, and not a lens

Constitution §6 asks one question: *what will it remember?* The answer is concrete and could not be
re-derived cold each session — **where students get lost, which materials rot every term, which
sequence orderings have already failed, and what Michael has refused to change.** That is a
semester-over-semester ledger, and a stateless lens would rebuild it wrong every August.

His `memory.md` Ledger C is therefore deliberately EMPTY at birth. It is the ledger that justifies the
class, so seeding it with guesses would have defeated the point.

## D5 · 2026-07-30 · Grading surfaces yes, grading judgement no

Tate keeps roster, attendance and assignment STRUCTURES honest. He does not decide what a student
earned. The line is deliberate: student academic records are the highest-consequence, lowest-
reversibility data in his world, and "the TA fixed the gradebook" is not a sentence anyone wants to
discover in an activity log. Safety, welfare and compliance paperwork stay wholly with Milo rather
than being half-held by both.
