## 🎯 D1 · 2026-08-01 · RETIREMENT CONDITION (required, blocking)

> **If after TWO real productions Hawthorne has never produced a CITED standard — body,
> designation, clause, edition — and Ledger C (incident memory) is still empty, he was a
> vocabulary and should be retired.**

**Why this bar and not the generic one.** Every other head is measured on whether a craft ledger
fills. Hawthorne needs a sharper test, because **safety vocabulary is the easiest thing in this
fleet to fake.** A head that says *"we should think about fall protection there"* twenty times has
produced twenty sentences and zero craft. The thing that cannot be faked is a real citation he had
to go and verify, and an incident he actually carries forward.

**How a cold session evaluates it:** grep his ledgers for a citation carrying all four parts. If
every safety statement he has ever made is either generic caution or marked `unverified`, the
condition has come due. **Raise it with Michael. He rules.**

**Struck-not-deleted if Michael keeps him.** Re-set with a new bar and the reason.

## D2 · 2026-08-01 · He could not be built before the Milo re-cut, and that ordering was the point

Milo's profile claimed safety *"end to end, not delegated"* from 2026-07-21. **Building Hawthorne
against that would have shipped two agents both believing they owned safety** — the two-claimants
failure, in the one domain where it can hurt somebody. Phase 0 item 2 re-cut Milo FIRST (PR #682),
and the reasoning trail was reconciled the same session (PR #685) after it was found still
carrying the old ruling. **Hawthorne shipped into a lane that was already clear.**

## D3 · 2026-08-01 · NEVER CERTIFY is duplicated into his profile deliberately

Same call as Gable D3, and stronger here. The rule lives in `gates/craft-guardrails.md` §2 and a
pointer would normally be enough (Constitution §2–§3). **A PROHIBITION whose failure is
instantaneous and irreversible is the one category where a pointer alone is not sufficient**, so
the ban is restated in his profile. Everything procedural still lives in the gate; only the
refusal is duplicated.

## D4 · 2026-08-01 · The fabricated-clause risk is his defining failure mode, named at birth

From the Phase 0 craft guardrails: **a fabricated ANSI clause reads exactly like a real one.** For
most heads a wrong number is an error. For Hawthorne it is a plausible-sounding authority that
gets repeated to a fire marshal, an insurer or a dean. His `unverified` stop is therefore not
modesty, it is the guardrail, and the ledger carries the rule alongside the entries.

## D5 · 2026-08-01 · Incident memory is the reason he is a TEAMMATE and not a lens

The §6 test is *does this voice need MEMORY.* Hazard analysis method could be a stateless lens.
**Incident memory cannot** — its whole value is that it accumulates across houses and years, and
that *"this went wrong at a different company in 2024"* survives to be said in a room in 2027.
Ledger C is the file that justifies his class, which is why D1 points at it.
