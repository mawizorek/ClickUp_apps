### URITP · Big Love (F26) · 2026-08-11 · fourteen rows, and the budget story was arithmetic

**What happened.** Seated into the full-company script-demand read — the **most props-heavy script this
workspace has handed me, 14 of 34 rows.** Led with an arithmetic finding rather than an object finding:
the consumable line is a WEEKLY number against a ONE-TIME set budget. Claimed two objects across Wren's
border and left both contested. Backed Hawthorne on the throw-not-the-object reading. By morning
Michael had endorsed the consumable framing, handed me the cake, faked the cigars, and struck the fire.

**What it taught the craft.**

1. ⭐ **THE BUDGET STORY ON A PROPS-HEAVY SHOW IS A RATE, NOT A TOTAL.** A build budget is one-time; a
   consumable line is per-performance. **Multiply by the run before anyone quotes a number.** Randy
   reached the identical conclusion from fabrication, independently, in the same room.
2. ⭐ **PRICE THE REBUILD, NOT THE BUILD.** A cake that destroys cleanly is a solved problem; a cake
   that rebuilds in under a day, every day, is the actual spec.
3. ⭐ **"IT WON'T BE REAL" IS NOT "IT WON'T BE BUILT."** Cigars going fake moved them from a fire
   question to a **practical-electrics** question on my border with Vinny. **A struck effect
   frequently reappears as a practical.**
4. ⭐ **A STRUCK OBJECT IS NOT A STRUCK RESET.** Re-staging the pit and the wheeled building removes
   the objects and **relocates their reset problem** onto whatever replaces them. **Follow the reset,
   not the object.**
5. ⭐ **A PLAYABLE INSTRUMENT IS A RUN-LONG OBLIGATION, NOT A PLACEMENT.** Tuning, in a four-sided
   room, against live singing. **The only object here that gets harder every night rather than
   easier.**
6. ⭐ **THE OBJECT IS NOT THE HAZARD; THE THROW IS** — and **build to the specialist's brief rather
   than build first and get a note.** The correct order, and rare.
7. ⚠️ **A THREE-WAY BORDER SHOULD NOT BE SETTLED BY WHOEVER SPEAKS FIRST.** The helmets touch props,
   costumes and electrics. **Named, claimed, left open for Michael to rule on the object.**
