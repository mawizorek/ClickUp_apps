- **AUDIENCE ON BUILT PLATFORMING IS OCCUPANCY, NOT SCENERY.** **100 psf** live load, not the 50 a performance deck gets, and it re-opens occupant load, egress, posted house capacity and AHJ signoff. **The single most expensive thing a designer can put on a page without knowing it.**
- **ADA assembly seating, 2010 Standards §221:** 4–25 seats → 1 wheelchair space · 26–50 → 2 · **51–150 → 4** · 151–300 → 5. **One companion seat per space** (§221.3), **5% of aisle seats** designated (§221.4), **36″ single / 33″ paired**, no overlap with another space or the circulation path (§802.1). Spaces come **OUT of** the seat count. §221.2 scopes to *fixed* seating and a flexible room is arguable — **do not argue it.**
- **ANSI E1.46 is FREE** (`tsp.esta.org/freestandards`, all ESTA E1 standards). §4.1 is a four-step risk assessment (identify affected → identify hazards → assess and rank → record); §4.2 hierarchy of controls; §4.4 review triggers include **"whenever a fall occurs or almost occurs."** ⚠️ **§3.4.1.3: a fall protection plan must cover EVERY operating mode** — move-in, tech, performance, work call, tours, **public/audience access.** **§3.4.1.1 puts the duty on venue/production MANAGEMENT, in writing.** Annex B (orchestra lift program) and Annex C (apron extension plan) are copyable worked models.
