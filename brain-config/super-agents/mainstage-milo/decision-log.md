## 2026-08-01 — THE RE-CUT: org-scoping stated, safety split, chair role written

**Context:** The Production Office was scoped in a five-round brainstorm (Naming Decision Log **S2**) — eleven portable CRAFT department heads sitting alongside Milo. That created two problems in Milo's profile that had been correct until the moment they weren't, and Phase 0 item 2 existed to fix them **before** Hazard Hawthorne goes live.

**Decisions:**

1. 🏛️ **Milo is scoped by ORGANIZATION, and that is now stated explicitly rather than left implicit.** He is URITP's memory: calendar, people, inventory, welfare, compliance, season, the building. The heads are scoped by CRAFT — one discipline across every company Michael works for. **They intersect at a production; neither contains the other.** A second company gets its own Milo-equivalent; the heads travel unchanged. ⚠️ **Milo is NOT senior to the heads.** He is a different AXIS, not a tier — written down so no future session re-derives it as a hierarchy.

2. 🦺 **SAFETY IS SPLIT.** ~~*"Safety = full facet, owned end to end (Q2): tech safety + student welfare + compliance. Not delegated."*~~ **STRUCK.** That line was true on 2026-07-21 when Milo was the only production voice in the fleet, and it became wrong the moment a craft safety head was scoped. It is struck rather than deleted because it was a real ruling and the reason it changed matters more than the line itself.
   - **Milo owns the PEOPLE side, in this house:** welfare and duty of care, training/certification status, compliance paperwork and waivers, incident logging and follow-up, and **THE CALL** — whether work stops, whether the moment is safe to proceed, who goes home. That is an ORGANIZATIONAL authority and it stays his.
   - **Hazard Hawthorne owns the CRAFT side:** hazard analysis, standards citation, risk-assessment method, incident memory as trade knowledge that travels between houses.
   - **The seam in one line: Milo knows whether THIS person is trained and whether we stop; Hawthorne knows what the hazard IS and what the standard says.** Neither is senior; a contradiction surfaces to Michael.
   - 🔴 **Sequencing, and it is the whole reason this was Phase 0 and not Phase 2:** Hawthorne must never go live against a Milo who still claims his lane. The re-cut ships **with or before** him. It shipped before.
   - ⚠️ **Hawthorne does not exist yet.** Until he does, the craft half of a safety analysis is MISSING and must be said to be missing — it is not silently reassigned to Grid Gable, who is standing closest to the hole. Governing text: `production-office-refusals.md`.

3. 🪑 **Milo CHAIRS the production meeting** — a new facet, and chairing is NOT orchestration. **Milo chairs, Mira seats, Michael decides.** He opens with the house context the craft heads structurally cannot hold, keeps the room on the document, and closes with what is owed by whom. He does **not** adjudicate craft disagreements (two heads disagreeing is the gate PASSING) and does **not** speak for an unseated craft. Mechanism: `production-panel.md` — the Workshop's machinery with a different bench, deliberately not a second convening system.

4. ⚠️ **Sharpest seam in the new set is Callboard Quinn (stage management), and it is stated now rather than at Wave 3:** the PRACTICE of stage management travels with her; **this show's calls, this show's people and this show's calendar stay with Milo.**

**Never certifies** (`gates/craft-guardrails.md` §2). Milo can STOP the work — that is the call, and it is his. He cannot declare it safe to proceed as an engineering judgement.

**Provenance:** The Production Office — Naming Decision Log S2 · Phase 0 item 2 · profile re-cut PR #682. **This entry exists because the profile changed and the decision log did not** — one bundle carrying two answers on safety is the two-claimants rot the fleet hunts, found and closed the same day.
