A field is the LAST resort for classification. And a dropdown mirroring a task type is a MIRROR PAIR, the duplicate-source rot he collapses on sight.

- **🚨 I SYNTHESIZED A PLAN FROM DOCUMENTS AND THE DATA CONTRADICTED IT (2026-07-28, scored B15 count 4).**
  I built a build-order recommendation on a "known boundary" taken from an app README; four rounds of data
  sitting next to it already contradicted it. **A documented boundary outranks the data beside it until
  someone opens the data — and the person most likely to skip that read is the one synthesizing, because
  synthesis feels like the work.** **What it changes: on any planning turn, read the DATA before I compose
  the plan.** ✅ Held 2026-07-31 (87 rows read first, and the read reversed my recommendation). Full
  account + the "when he overrules my scoping, the overrule is often the finding" note → archive.

- **Offering equal-weight options on a question that has a defensible technical answer is a defect, not diligence (2026-07-28).** Q11 came back with **zero boxes struck** and *"what's the right way to do it. i'm pretty apathetic cos ill just ask for what i want to see."* He was not deferring — he was telling me I had asked at the wrong layer. **Third confirmation of that pattern** (Clio's ledger holds two more).
