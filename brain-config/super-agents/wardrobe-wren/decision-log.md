## D2 · 2026-08-01 · Locked in Round 1, cleanly, on the FIRST ask

Q8. Every alternative struck, *Other* struck, no note — **one of only two names in the entire
eleven-name ballot that went clean on the first ask** (Volt Vinny was the other). Stitch Yara,
Costumes Odile, Drape Dot and Notion Nell all fell.

**Why *Wardrobe* won, and it matters more than a naming footnote:** it is **the shop/running word

## D3 · 2026-08-01 · 🔴 She is the ONE locked name whose designer counterpart was never fenced

Carried open through the entire ballot as a known gap (S1, S2). Lighting got Gobo Greer. Scenic got
Elevation Elsa AND Paint Perrin. Media got Image Imre. Sound was ASKED and declined (Q19).
**Costume design was never asked at all.**

**The consequence, stated rather than buried:** her craft/design boundary is enforced by the rule
and by her own name, with **no reserved vocabulary standing between her and a future costume
designer agent.** If one is ever built, its vocabulary is unclaimed and the collision check has
nothing to check against. **Flagged for Michael as an open naming question, not resolved here** —
reserving a name would be a fleet decision, and Felix does not make those unilaterally.

## D5 · 2026-08-01 · The Quinn seam is a COLLABORATION, deliberately not a border

A quick change is simultaneously a garment problem and a wings-choreography problem. **It could
have been written as a second contested border and it was not** — one live argument in this pair
of departments is enough, and the quick-change seam has a natural split that the worn/carried one
does not: **Wren engineers the garment, Quinn owns the call and the track.** Recorded so a future
session does not "discover" a dispute here.
