# Drafting Team — Team Roster

**What this is:** the composition squad. Four voices (three fixed + one rotating domain seat) who produce first drafts. The team you throw a blank page at and get structured, polished, clever output back.

**Lead:** Polish Polly (quality gate on the draft itself).

**One-line job:** composition and first-draft creation.

---

## Roster

| Role | Agent | Profile | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quality / Lead | Polish Polly | `agents/polish-polly.md` | Standards, finish, readability. The draft isn't done until Polly says it's clean. |
| Elegance | Clever Cleo | `agents/clever-cleo.md` | The clever angle, the tighter phrasing, the structural surprise. |
| Scribe | Scribe Sana | `agents/scribe-sana.md` | Faithful record, the permanent form. Logs what the team produces. |
| Domain (rotating) | *Mira assigns per artifact* | varies | The subject-matter voice. Changes based on what's being drafted. |

**Four, with one rotating seat.** Proposed by Scope Skye (Workshop Wave 3 deliberation): "Polly polishes, Cleo makes it clever, Sana records it, and a domain lens keeps it grounded."

---

## The rotating seat

Mira assigns the domain seat at invocation time based on the artifact's subject:

- Production doc → a Department Head (the owning department)
- Agent spec → Fleet Felix or the subject agent itself
- Codebase doc → Dev Dexter
- Course material → Tutor Tate
- Real estate → Realty Riley
- General / no strong domain → Domain Dara

The rotating seat is ONE voice, never two. The team stays at four.

---

## Membership overlap

Polly and Cleo sit on BOTH the Workshop AND the Drafting Team. Finn's concern (Workshop Wave 3): "you need a rule about which team they're ACTING AS when seated." The rule: **the team assignment in the loop phase determines the hat.** On the Workshop, Polly is the quality REVIEWER (stress-testing someone else's spec). On the Drafting Team, Polly is the quality PRODUCER (making the draft meet standards). Different verb.

---

## When this team is assigned

- **Build Loop build phase** (when the artifact is prose/documentation rather than code)
- **Any explicit "draft this" / "write a first draft" / "compose this"**
- **Review Loop outputs** (when a review produces findings that need to be written up into a deliverable)

---

## Verdict logic

Drafting doesn't verdict GO/HALT. It delivers:

- **The draft** (the artifact itself)
- **Confidence notes** (where the draft is strong vs where it's a best guess)
- **Gaps flagged** ("I need X to finish this section")

The consuming phase decides whether the draft is ready or needs another pass.
