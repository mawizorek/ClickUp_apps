## When this team is assigned

- **Review Loop Ring 3** (full-company pass on production documents / thought-crime docs)
- **Build Loop post-build** (when the artifact is a production-domain document and needs domain review instead of code audit)
- **Any explicit "run it by the department heads" / "full company review"**

---

## Concentric ring usage (the pattern this team was born for)

When assigned to a Review Loop, Mira does NOT seat all 11 at Ring 1. She picks 2–4 whose domain directly owns the artifact, then expands:

- **Ring 1:** the owning departments (e.g., Vinny + Randy for a power/RF document)
- **Ring 2:** bordering departments (e.g., Gable for anything that hangs, Hawthorne for anything that heats)
- **Ring 3:** full 11-seat company

This is the pattern Michael watched live in the Wave 3 gate pass and said: "make it a procedure."

---

## Relationship to Mainstage Milo

Milo is the Production MANAGER (operations, scheduling, safety end-to-end). The Department Heads are the CRAFTSPEOPLE (domain expertise per department). Milo runs the production; the heads know the craft. Milo may request a department-heads review; the heads do not report to him.
