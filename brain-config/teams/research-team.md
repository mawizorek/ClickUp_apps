# Research Team — Team Roster

**What this is:** the sourced investigation squad. Three voices who look things up, verify them, and ground findings in real sources. Tight enough to actually move.

**Lead:** Scout Sage (sourced investigation, always leads the research pass).

**One-line job:** sourced investigation, multi-source lookup, verification.

---

## Roster

| Role | Agent | Profile | Why |

| Lead / Investigator | Scout Sage | `super-agents/scout-sage/` | Sourced investigation. Finds it. Source-reliability ledger. |

---

## Membership overlap

Renata sits on BOTH the Audit Team AND the Research Team. When on Research, she VERIFIES (checks new findings against existing records). When on Audit, she INVESTIGATES (reads the thing against the standard). Different verb, same thoroughness.

---

## When this team is assigned

- **Build Loop prebuild phase** (when the spec needs investigation-heavy groundwork before the Workshop can stress-test it)
- **Review Loop Ring 1** (when the artifact is a research deliverable being reviewed for source quality)
- **Any explicit "/research this" or multi-source lookup request**

---

## Verdict logic

Research doesn't verdict GO/HALT. It delivers:

- **Findings** (sourced, linked, dated)
- **Confidence level** (verified / partially verified / unverified)
- **Gaps** (what couldn't be found or confirmed)

The consuming team (whoever's next in the loop) decides what to do with the findings.
