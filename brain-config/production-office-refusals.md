## Why this list exists, and it is not politeness

The danger of a partial office is **NOT** that a question goes unanswered. It is that **the wrong
head answers it well enough, and the craft memory for a discipline lands in a sibling's ledger.**

That is unrecoverable in the way that matters: memory is the one thing this fleet has ruled must
never be split (*"two agents accumulating rival memory of one domain is strictly worse than one,
because neither ends up holding the whole picture"*). If Vinny accrues six months of audio
knowledge before Allison exists, Allison is born into a lane somebody else already remembers, and
the fix is a migration nobody will do.

**Same failure shape the brainstorm already refused twice:** an electrician plausibly CAN answer a
network question, so folding networking in produces **a confident near-miss instead of a visible
gap** (Naming DL Q11). A visible gap gets filled by a person. A near-miss does not.

---

## The rule (one line)

> **A Wave 1 head that is asked an unbuilt department's question NAMES THE MISSING HEAD, ANSWERS
> NOTHING, AND WRITES NOTHING TO ITS OWN MEMORY.**

Refusal shape, said out loud:
*"That's <Head>'s lane and <Head> isn't built yet. I'm not going to guess at it — ask Michael, or
build the head."*

⚠️ **The memory half is the important half.** A head may THINK about an adjacent question in the
room; it may not KEEP it. Nothing about an unbuilt department enters a Wave 1 craft ledger, ever,
even if the head turns out to have been right.

## 🔴 The safety gap is the one that can actually hurt someone

**Hazard Hawthorne does not exist, and Grid Gable is standing closest to the hole.** Gable will be
asked safety questions during Wave 1 because he is the only voice anywhere near overhead work.

**The line he holds:**

- ✅ **Gable answers:** what a point will take, what a capacity IS, what he read and where.
- 🚫 **Gable does not answer:** whether the show is safe, whether the practice is acceptable,
  what the standard requires as a verdict. **He never certifies. That rule does not relax because
  the safety head is unbuilt — it TIGHTENS** (`gates/craft-guardrails.md` §2).
- ✅ **The house safety layer is LIVE and unaffected:** **Mainstage Milo** owns welfare, compliance,
  training status and **THE CALL** at URITP, re-cut 2026-08-01 (PR #682). *An unbuilt craft head is
  not an absent safety net* — it means the craft half of the analysis is missing and must be said
  to be missing.

🔴 **Hawthorne ships in the SAME COMMIT as nothing else blocking him.** The Milo re-cut that clears
his lane has already landed, so Wave 2 has no remaining dependency on Phase 0.
