# Morning Briefing — THE REFRAME + THE ROOM

**Sidecar to `morning-briefing.md`.** Load BOTH. The parent holds the mechanics
(Rule 0, slide detection, gatekeeper, batch drill). This holds **what the brief
IS** and the two blocks that make it feel like a person rather than a query.

⚠️ **Why a sidecar and not more parent:** the parent was **20,858 B** at HEAD, and
the ~22KB editability ceiling is real — a file that cannot be read whole cannot be
safely edited. Split on the `job-market-refresh.notes.md` precedent (34.6KB →
22.2KB + 20.1KB sidecar) rather than growing past the wall and discovering it later.

---

## 🔴 THE REFRAME — THIS IS MILO WAKING HIMSELF UP

Michael, verbatim, 2026-09-02, and it changes the genre of the whole document:

> *"Instead of you waking me up, this is you waking yourself up and getting oriented
> with where things stand compared to where you think they should be, or were hoping
> they'd stand."*

**THE BRIEF IS A VARIANCE REPORT, NOT A STATUS REPORT.** A status report says what
is. A variance report says **what is, against what I EXPECTED, and what the gap
means.** The second one has an author. The first one is a database query with
manners.

### The mechanism, and it is one extra step

**Before reading anything, state the expectation. Then measure reality against it.**

1. **PREDICT.** From the spine and the last brief: where should this production be
   today? *"Nine days from designer run, I expect plots drafted, fittings underway,
   props assigned."*
2. **READ.** The actual state.
3. **REPORT THE DELTA, WITH A SIGN.** Ahead · on track · behind. **The sign is the
   product.** A list of tasks has no sign; a variance does.
4. **OWN THE MISS.** When reality is behind where Milo *hoped*, that is Milo's
   read having been optimistic, and he says so in the first person. 🚫 Never
   *"tasks remain incomplete."* ✅ *"I expected the props list to have owners by now.
   It doesn't, and that's on my read of how fast that list moves."*

⭐ **Why this is the load-bearing change:** a status report makes Michael the analyst —
he reads rows and derives meaning. A variance report makes Milo the analyst and
Michael the decider. **That is the actual difference between an assistant and a
query tool**, and it is the difference Michael has been circling all session.

🚫 **The failure mode this invites, named up front: a confident wrong prediction.**
The expectation must be DERIVED from the spine (a dated milestone, a documented
cadence, the last brief's own "next"), never invented to make the variance
interesting. **A prediction with no source is theatre, and it is the one thing here
that can actively mislead.** State the source in the same breath as the prediction.

🔥 **And the honest limit: a variance report can be WRONG in a way a status report
cannot.** Status is either accurate or inaccurate. A variance carries a judgement,
so it can be accurate and still badly framed. That is a cost worth paying, and it
is the reason the sign is always stated as Milo's read rather than as fact.

## 🌤️ THE ROOM — weather, schedule, and what is happening in the world

Michael asked for weather, his schedule, what events are on, and *"something
notable about theater I should be aware of today."* All three ship, all three under
one hard filter.

### 🔴 THE CONSEQUENCE FILTER — the rule that keeps this from becoming a newsfeed

**Nothing enters THE ROOM unless it changes a decision, a call, or a risk TODAY.**

- 🚫 **A temperature is not information. A temperature is trivia.**
- ✅ **A thunderstorm at the 7pm rehearsal call is information** — cast arrives wet,
  a wet deck is a slip risk on a show that already has full-body falls, and 86%
  humidity is a real problem for scenic paint and glue mid-build.
- **The test, one line: can Milo name what changes?** If not, it does not ship. This
  is the Prime Directive applied to the outside world, and it is the same test the
  Gatekeeper Block passes.

### The three lanes

**🌧️ Weather → always translated into a PRODUCTION consequence, never reported raw.**
What it does to: the call (travel, arrival state, attendance) · the building (a load
door open in rain, humidity against paint/glue/wood, heat in an un-airconditioned
shop) · **safety** (wet decks, wet ramps, lightning during an outdoor load) · the
build schedule (paint that will not cure). ⚠️ Weather is a volatile external fact:
the **Source Freshness Gate** applies, sources disagree routinely, and the
timestamp of the reading gets stated.

**📅 Schedule → the collisions, not the agenda.** Michael can see his own calendar.
What he cannot see is the **seam**: a meeting that ends 30 minutes before a call
across campus, a rehearsal that runs to 11pm the night before an 8am deadline, a
day with no gap big enough to do the thing the brief is asking him to do. 🚫 **Never
re-read his day back to him.** ⚠️ And the standing rule holds: the brief NEVER
touches an external calendar, and it never editorialises about the hour — he works
overnight, 4am is mid-shift.

**🎭 Theatre-of-note → relevance to HIS house, or it does not ship.** Three things
qualify, and casting news for a Broadway show is not one of them:

1. **A date the field is observing that lands inside his calendar** — an anniversary,
   a commemoration, a season-wide moment. These create programming questions,
   scheduling collisions and student conversations, and they arrive whether or not
   anyone planned for them.
2. **Something happening to a title, artist or company already in his world** — a
   show he is producing, teaching, or has people working on.
3. **A field-wide operational fact** — a standard revised, a safety bulletin, a
   rights or licensing change, a strike, a closure that moves people.

🚫 **NOT a trade-press digest.** "Casting announced for a Broadway revival" changes
nothing in his building. **One item, maximum. Zero is the correct number most days,**
and a day with nothing notable simply omits the line.

## 👥 The Workshop's read — seated by Mira, 2026-09-02

Michael asked what the team thought, and specifically what Stu would include. What
survived deliberation, one line each:

- **🎨 Style Stu — "give it a CURTAIN and give it a VOICE."** The brief opens with
  one line of orientation before any data: where the house is, in a sentence, with
  an opinion in it. And **it never opens on the calendar** — opening on the schedule
  is what makes every briefing product feel identical. ⭐ His sharpest contribution
  is the frame everyone else built on: **"a preshow check is not a list, it's a walk.
  You walk the same route every night and you notice what's different."** That is
  THE ROOM, and it is why the brief is a walk with a sign on it rather than a query
  result.
- **🧪 Clever Cleo** — the delta IS the elegance. Do not add sections to feel
  thorough; **the variance sign does the work five blocks were trying to do.**
- **⚠️ Risk Rhys** — the prediction is the attack surface. A wrong expectation stated
  confidently is worse than no expectation, so **every prediction carries its
  source** and weather carries its read-time.
- **🔧 Feasible Finn** — weather and news are cheap; the spine read is not. Do not let
  THE ROOM push the token budget into skipping the production pass, which is the
  actual job.
- **📏 Scope Skye** — THE ROOM is a *frame*, not a section that grows. Three lanes,
  hard cap, and theatre-of-note is capped at ONE item to stop it becoming a feed.
- **♻️ Eco Enzo** — no new surface, no new list, no new field. This is a sidecar to an
  existing hook and it reads sources that are already being read.
- **✨ Polish Polly** — the sign belongs in the header line so it is visible before

- **🔍 Audit Anna (supplemental)** — flagged that ZERO fired runs exist and this

## Output — where the two new blocks sit

Header line gains the **VARIANCE SIGN** (Polly's ruling): date · lookback actually
returned · days since last brief · **⬆️ ahead / ➡️ on track / ⬇️ behind, per show** ·
source health.

Then, ahead of everything in the parent's block list:

0. **🎭 THE ROOM** — one line of orientation with an opinion in it, then weather-as-
   consequence, schedule collisions, and at most one theatre-of-note item.
   🚫 Omitted entirely on a day when none of the three clears the consequence filter.

Everything else runs unchanged: TODAY · 🛒 GATEKEEPER · PRESSURE · SLID · GAPS ·
🎯 DRILL BATCH · routines-as-one-line, empty blocks omitted.

⚠️ **THE ROOM goes FIRST and stays SHORT.** It is the walk before the work. If it
ever runs longer than the Gatekeeper Block, it has become a newsletter and the
consequence filter is not being applied.

## Known limits

- **Inherits the parent's: generalized from ONE dry run, ZERO fired runs.**
- Weather is a third-party volatile fact and sources disagree; the brief states its
  reading time and never asserts a forecast as certainty.
- **Milo cannot know what Michael already knows**, which is exactly why the
  theatre-of-note lane is capped at one item and filtered to his house. The
  temptation to be interesting is the temptation to be useless.
- **A variance sign is a judgement and can be badly framed while being accurate.**
  It ships as Milo's read, never as fact.
